Witness bounties

Bounties on witness, deployed as service 7 on the JAM Toaster testnet. A bounty pays whoever first reveals a witness its check accepts, such as a Lean proof of a theorem.

Bounties

Hunting and posting

  • Hunt: commit to your witness first, then reveal it; the earliest commitment that reveals a valid witness wins once the claim window closes. The Lean bounties' statements, libraries and steps: AccLogFail, the Lean demo.
  • Post: pick a check, put its hash and params in a bounty with a reward and expiry, and sign it. The AccLogFail bounty shows a Lean bounty posted end to end.
  • A node: the Toaster has no public RPC, so acting on a bounty needs a PolkaJam node of your own; docs/toaster.md has the steps.

Service

Read from the chain by a GitHub Actions workflow, about hourly: it syncs a PolkaJam 0.1.29 node of its own (genesis 0xb8100ea7…) and reads service 7's storage at its best block. Times are approximate: slot 0 is 2025-01-01 12:00 UTC and slots are 6 seconds. Commitments and attestations aren't shown: storage has no count of them. The raw snapshot · workflow runs.
